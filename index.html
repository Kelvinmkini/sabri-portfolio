About Me

A passionate technologist bridging the worlds of data science and cloud engineering.

I am Sabri Fujo, a dedicated data science undergraduate with a strong academic background in statistics at the Eastern Africa Statistical Training Center (EASTC). Having earned both a Certificate in Statistics (2021) and a Diploma in Statistics (2023) from EASTC, I have built a rigorous quantitative foundation that underpins my approach to data analysis, modelling, and engineering.

My journey into technology is driven by a genuine curiosity about how data shapes decisions and how cloud systems enable scalable solutions. I am particularly passionate about the intersection of statistical thinking and modern software engineering — applying mathematical rigour to real-world cloud-native applications.

Currently exploring cloud computing, full-stack development, and machine learning pipelines, I aspire to build systems that are not only technically robust but also impactful — solving meaningful problems at scale for businesses and communities.

I believe in clean architecture, continuous learning, and the philosophy that the best solutions emerge at the intersection of data intelligence and thoughtful engineering.
